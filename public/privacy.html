Baka To-do List Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Information stored

When you sign in with Google, the app stores your Firebase user ID, display name, email address, profile image URL, and last sign-in timestamp. Tasks you create are stored in Cloud Firestore under your user account.

How information is used

This information is used to authenticate you, display your account, synchronize your tasks, and enforce per-user access controls. The app does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Authentication and storage are provided by Firebase and Google. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

Retention and deletion

You can use the account deletion control in the signed-in app to permanently remove your tasks, profile document, and Firebase Auth account. Recent authentication may be required. For additional deletion requests, contact the project owner through the repository or deployment contact.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the person or organization that operates this deployment.
